# Frequently asked questions

[Back to the project overview](../README.md)

## Does the marker follow every movement automatically?

The marker updates when the app receives new Asset Location coordinates. With key-triggered reading, press your game key to show the panel. With automatic reading, keep those coordinates visible. A hidden panel means there is no fresh screen-reading position to capture.

## Can I see my friends on official servers?

Yes. Friends using active Squad access can join the same private squad and share their latest captured positions while playing Gateway on official or community servers. These are your squad members’ shared coordinates, not a feed of other players on the server.

## Is Tab required?

Tab is the default. If you changed the in-game key that opens the panel containing Asset Location, choose the matching key in **Settings > Position Updates**. Clipboard updates are another option.

## Does it read game memory?

No. Position updates use copied coordinates or a screen area you configure. The app does not inject into the game, read game memory or modify game files.

## Is the app free or open source?

TheIsleMinimap is a licensed, proprietary Windows application. This repository contains public guides and screenshots, not the app source. Plans help support ongoing development, maintenance and online features. [View current plans](https://theisleminimap.com/pricing/).

## Does everyone need to purchase separately?

Squad Monthly and Squad Quarterly include four licenses, so one purchaser can invite three friends. Each friend needs their own free account and accepts their invitation on the Licenses page. Squad Lifetime and Solo Lifetime each include one license.

## Does Solo Lifetime work completely offline?

Solo tools do not share positions online, but the app still requires an internet connection for activation and periodic license checks.

## What happens if I cancel a subscription?

Cancel from **Account Portal > Billing**. Cancellation stops the next renewal; access continues through the current paid period. Lifetime plans do not renew.

## Where should I ask for help?

Use [Discord](https://discord.gg/WfxM6aEuWw), the [tutorial](https://theisleminimap.com/tutorial/) or a [private account support ticket](https://theisleminimap.com/account/?action=tickets). Keep keys, private squad codes, passwords and payment information out of public posts.
